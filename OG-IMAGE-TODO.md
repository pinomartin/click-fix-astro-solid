# OG Image TODO

You need to create an `og-image.jpg` file in the `public/` directory.

## Specifications:

- **Size**: 1200x630 pixels (required for Facebook/Twitter)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Content**: Should include:
  - Click & Fix logo
  - Company tagline: "Soluciones digitales que encajan"
  - Your brand colors (accent: #d06c56)
  - Clean, professional design

## Tools you can use:

1. **Canva** (easiest): https://www.canva.com/
   - Search for "Facebook Post" or "Twitter Post" templates
   - Customize with your branding
2. **Figma**: Create a 1200x630px frame and design

3. **Photoshop/GIMP**: Professional tools

## Quick Tips:

- Keep text large and readable (social previews are small)
- Use high contrast between text and background
- Center important content (edges may be cropped)
- Test on Facebook Sharing Debugger: https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/
- Test on Twitter Card Validator: https://cards-dev.twitter.com/validator

Once created, save it as `/public/og-image.jpg`
